What makes it safe to demo live

  1. Reset demo button in the top banner. It puts everything back to Wed Sep 23 in under 5 seconds, so you can rehearse and still start clean on stage.
  2. Real AI, with a safety net. The sample sheets run through the real vision model and the real name matching. If the venue Wi-Fi stalls, the app falls back to the verified reading of that same sheet and keeps going.
  3. Frozen clock. The demo always thinks it is Wed Sep 23, 2026, so "this week" and the 4 calls never drift.
  4. Sample sheets pre-tested. Each one is checked through the live pipeline during the build until every planted name resolves correctly.
  5. Two ways in. The deployed link, plus a local copy on your laptop as backup.

Welcome page and first screen

  1. A real landing page for judges. It says what it does in one line, shows the three steps (Snap, Call, Prove), the before and after, Marisol's quote, and the tech in plain words.
  2. Today opens with a three-step plan. Step 1 Snap today's sheet, step 2 Call 4 families, step 3 Q1 report ready. Each step has one big button.
  3. Demo banner on every screen says the date is frozen and the data is fictional, with Reset.
  4. Snap starts with "Start here". The Sep 23 Citrus Grove sheet is highlighted so nobody on stage hunts for it.
