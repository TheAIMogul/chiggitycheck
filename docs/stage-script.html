Stage script · 2 minutes

Explainer, then the live demo at attendclear.com. Every step below was run on the live site.

Before you walk up
  • Open attendclear.com in Chrome. Press F for full screen.
  • Click Reset in the top banner once (it is on every app page). Everything returns to Wed Sep 23 with 3 families to call.
  • Have the printed Citrus Grove sheet on the lectern. Keep your phone on the same URL as a backup.
  • Audience: tell them to scan the QR code on slide 3 later. It opens the sign-up form.
0:00

Slide 1 · The problem

Press → to advance

"Attendance lives on paper. Marisol, a program manager, spends 5 to 15 hours a week retyping sign-in sheets. A kid can miss three sessions before anyone calls home, because the sheets aren't typed in yet. And every quarter the funder report takes three days, and the numbers never match."

0:20

Slide 2 · What it does (animation plays on its own)

"Take a photo of the sheet. It reads every name, matches each spelling to the right kid, tells you who to call this week with the message already written in the family's language, and keeps the funder report finished. Every number opens the photo it came from."

0:40

Slide 3 · How that fixes it

Click Start the demo

"No more retyping. No kid slips through. No report crunch. Let me show you. It's Wednesday, 4:40 pm, Citrus Grove Middle."

0:50

Today · Snap the sheet

Click Snap sheet in step 1. Hold up the paper sheet while it reads (about 12 seconds).

"This is a real photo of today's sheet. The AI is reading Luis's handwriting right now. Fourteen names."

1:05

Review

Point at the two questions. Click Add a new kid for Yaneli, then Yes, here for Daniel. Click Save attendance.

"Twelve matched above ninety-five percent. 'Dariel Rmz' is Dariel Ramírez, and it just learned that spelling. One kid we've never seen, so it asks. One check mark the camera wasn't sure about, so it asks. That's it. Save."

1:20

Watch it ripple

The save screen shows what changed. Click See the calls.

"Fourteen lines saved, the new spellings learned, two hours logged for Luis. And Dariel just moved to Urgent: three Wednesdays in a row. Nobody typed anything."

1:30

Calls · Dariel

Click Send on WhatsApp. Wait for the ticks, then the reply (about 20 seconds). While it arrives, switch the language control to English and back.

"The message is already written in Spanish, from these facts and nothing else. The dates, the mentor's name, the next session. Sent. Delivered. Read. And here's mom's reply. Transportation. Dariel is coming next week. Nobody got left behind."

1:50

Prove it's real · edit one square

Open People → Dariel. Tap the Sep 2 square, choose Missed.

"This isn't a slideshow. Watch: I change one attendance square, and the app tells me everything else that moved. His rate, his risk level, the calls count, the report."

2:05

Report · click 86%

Open Report. Click Showed up. Click a sheet row.

"The quarterly report for the funder. Already done. Click any number, and there's the sheet it came from, with the line highlighted. Marisol got three days back. The funder got proof."

2:20

Close

Back to slide 3 (press Home key on the site root, or just say it). Point at the QR.

"AttendClear. Snap the sheet. Everything else is done. Scan the code and sign up a family. Watch it land in the records without creating a duplicate."

If something goes wrong

  • Read takes too long or fails: the app falls back to the verified reading of that same sheet and says so in a small note. Keep talking.
  • Wi-Fi dies: use your phone's hotspot. The site is on Cloudflare's edge; nothing runs on your laptop.
  • Someone already captured the Sep 23 sheet: press Reset in the banner. It takes under a second.
  • Backup links: check.micahberkley.com and chiggitycheck.micahberkley.workers.dev serve the same app.
  • Dataset builder: the avatar menu → Build a dataset. Type "Add a Saturday soccer program at Miami Jackson with 12 kids for 6 weeks, three are slipping." About 20 seconds. New kids, sessions, calls and report numbers appear.
